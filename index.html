How to use

Five steps from chart to trade.

Everything the indicator does. Everything you have to do.

1

Open TradingView, pick your instrument

Open TradingView and load any chart — XAUUSD, BTCUSD, US30, or any forex pair. Trading Mastery works on every instrument TradingView supports.

2

Choose your timeframe

Scalping? Use M15. Intraday? Use H1. The indicator adapts to your trading style — same dashboard, same scoring, every timeframe.

3

Check the entry — has the market moved?

Look at the chart. Has price already passed the suggested entry? Skip it — that setup is gone. Wait for the next signal.

4

Read the score on the dashboard

Look at the Trading Mastery dashboard. Score 5/7.0 or higher? That's a valid setup — you have a green light to enter.

5

Enter on MT5 with SL & TP

Open MT5, enter the trade using the exact Entry, Stop Loss, TP1 and TP2 shown by the indicator. Set your lot size based on your risk plan.
